## 3. Storage & Delivery Specifications

- **Dual-Format Delivery:** Every image asset is published in both **WebP** (modern high-efficiency format) and **JPEG** (universal fallback).
- **Standard Dimensions:** All photographic hero and card banners are normalized to 1200x800 pixels (3:2 ratio) using high-precision Lanczos resampling.
- **Serving Path:** Public website assets are served from `/assets/images/` with WebP auto-resolution enabled via `src/data/businessData.ts`.
- **License Compliance:** 100% commercial usage rights with zero licensing restrictions (Pexels and Unsplash Free Commercial Licenses). Zero AI-generated imagery.
