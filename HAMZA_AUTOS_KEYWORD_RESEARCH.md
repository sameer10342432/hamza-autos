## 1. Keyword Strategy Overview

In Lahore, Pakistan, motorcycle search queries fall into three distinct user intent brackets:
1. **High-Intent Emergency Queries:** Riders experiencing immediate breakdowns, flat tyres, clutch cable snapping, or engine stalling on the road.
2. **Scheduled Maintenance Queries:** Commuters looking for periodic oil changes, carburetor tuning, tappet adjustments, and fuel mileage optimization.
3. **Model & Brand Specific Queries:** Owners of specific bikes (Honda CD 70, CG 125, Yamaha YBR 125, Suzuki GS 150) seeking experienced mechanics with genuine spare parts.
