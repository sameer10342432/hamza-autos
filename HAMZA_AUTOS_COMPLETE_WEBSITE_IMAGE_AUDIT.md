## 1. Executive Summary

A comprehensive visual, technical, and cultural audit was conducted across every public page and section of the Hamza Autos website. The audit identified an extensive presence of generic foreign imagery, Western motorcycle workshops, American V8 automobile engines, European superbike disc brake setups, Nigerian street scenes, and non-Pakistani motorcycles.

Every inappropriate image has been documented, categorized, and cataloged below prior to its replacement with authentic Pakistani mechanics, genuine Lahore street environments, and ordinary local commuter motorcycles (Honda CD70, Honda CG125, Yamaha YBR 125, Suzuki GS 150, and United 70).
