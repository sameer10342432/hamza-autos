# Hamza Autos — Advanced Business Analytics Documentation
**Location:** Workshop Operations Portal, Abu Bakar Road, Badami Bagh, Lahore  
**Architecture:** Real-time Aggregation over File-Based JSON Stores  
**Route:** `/admin/reports` and `/admin/dashboard`

---

## 1. Analytics Architecture
Hamza Autos generates high-fidelity operational and business intelligence directly from actual JSON records:
- Zero fake metrics or hardcoded figures.
- Dynamic time-filtering: Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, All Time.
- Instantaneous aggregations performed with asynchronous streaming and caching.

---

## 2. Key Analytical Dimensions

### A. Executive Workshop Operations
- **Repair Turnaround Time (TAT):** Average elapsed hours from `Received` timestamp to `Ready for Delivery`.
- **Active Bay Utilization:** Real-time occupancy of repair bays against total workshop capacity.
- **Completion Velocity:** Ratio of successfully delivered bikes to newly accepted job cards.

### B. Customer Retention & Repeat Business
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** Sum of paid invoices partitioned by customer ID.
- **Repeat Visit Rate:** Percentage of customers with $\ge 2$ bookings or job cards.
- **Service Reminder Conversion:** Rate of automated WhatsApp maintenance reminders turning into confirmed repair bookings.

### C. Revenue & Parts Profitability
- **Top Revenue Services:** Ranking by aggregate revenue (e.g. Engine Overhauling, General Tuning, Fork Straightening).
- **Fast-Moving Inventory:** Spare parts with highest turnover velocity (e.g. 20W-50 Engine Oil, Spark Plugs, Brake Shoes, Air Filters).
- **Parts Margin Analysis:** Percentage margin realized on parts sales ($[\text{Selling Price} - \text{Purchase Cost}] / \text{Selling Price}$).

### D. Technician & Bay Performance
- **Jobs Completed per Mechanic:** Productivity breakdown by Ustad / technician.
- **Rework & Warranty Rate:** Jobs returned within 14 days of completion.
- **Mechanic Wage Realization:** Ratio of labor revenue generated by mechanic compared to wages paid.

## 3. Reporting Export Formats
All analytics data, audit summaries, and operational tables can be:
- Printed directly in formatted tabular layout (`window.print()`).
- Exported as raw JSON data payloads for spreadsheet ingestion.
